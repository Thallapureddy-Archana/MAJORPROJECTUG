DoS (Denial of Service) Attack Precautions

Denial of Service (DoS) attacks aim to make a system or network resource unavailable to its users. To prevent DoS attacks:

  • Implement rate limiting to control traffic flow.
  • Use anti-DDoS services to mitigate large-scale attacks.
  • Ensure redundant network infrastructure to maintain availability.
  • Regularly update security systems to handle new threats.
