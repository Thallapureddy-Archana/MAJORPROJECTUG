R2L (Remote to Local) Attack Precautions

Remote to Local (R2L) attacks occur when an attacker sends packets to a machine over a network and gains local access. To prevent R2L attacks:

  • Disable unnecessary services and ports.
  • Use firewalls to block unauthorized access.
  • Implement intrusion detection systems (IDS) to monitor network traffic.
  • Educate users about phishing and other social engineering tactics.
